# Game visual fidelity

Applies to visual work on every new or existing Arcade game. Each game's approved
design controls its aesthetic; Ghost Circuit is an example, not a shared theme.
This standard adds no implementation, purchase, content-expansion or release
authority and does not initiate audits or restyling of other games.

## Establish the visual contract

Open the actual approved concept/reference files before implementing and during
runtime QA. In the game's existing DESIGN or equivalent design record, identify
the source and approved aspects, then record concrete must-preserve composition,
proportions, character prominence, materials, typography, palette, lighting, mood,
camera and motion where applicable. State target sizes/aspect ratios and tolerated
differences, including responsive and accessibility adaptations. Matching colors
alone is insufficient. Missing files, unclear approval and technical constraints
must be reported explicitly; do not invent approval or silently redesign.

## Prove the direction early

The first representative playable scene must already be directionally faithful
before content expansion or bulk art. Budget and produce the appropriate assets
within the authorized milestone, with usable runtime exports and provenance.
Do not silently replace a prominent character, physical material or composition
with generic panels or placeholders. Explicitly owner-scoped minimal/placeholder
prototypes remain valid within their stated scope. A failed proof needs concrete
in-scope corrections; a material scope or feasibility conflict follows the active
issue workflow.

## Compare the actual runtime

Capture the running game at its target device sizes/aspect ratios, including
desktop and phone when supported. Cover relevant planning, selection, failure
and success states only where the game and changed area have them. Open the real
captures beside the approved references: inspect overall composition and scale,
then character, materials, typography, readability and interaction details.
Observe actual playback when motion is present; a still cannot prove animation.
Correct concrete deviations autonomously within the approved scope, and record
remaining differences, constraints and unverified checks honestly.

Keep functional and visual acceptance separate. Passing tests, dimensions or a
build does not establish visual fidelity, playability or movement quality. Do not
claim visual PASS while a meaningful required attribute remains mismatched.

## Review and evidence reuse

The independent reviewer already required by `AI_ISSUE_WORKFLOW.md` must open the
approved references and actual runtime images for affected visual work. Meaningful
mismatch blocks visual acceptance; use the existing risk lane, correction process
and roles without an extra committee or owner approval ceremony.

Index evidence against the project criteria and candidate footprint using the
active issue workflow. Rendering, assets, layout, client state and other relevant
changes invalidate affected captures. Reuse valid observations with their original
identity and reason; unchanged backend or docs-only work does not force recapture.
No additional walkthrough is required solely because these instructions changed.
